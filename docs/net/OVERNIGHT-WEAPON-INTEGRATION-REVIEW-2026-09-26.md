## Cross-layer invariants inspected

- VERIFIED — Rail discharge in `controller.ts` requires the exact current native press's assigned intent sequence, successful intent result, raw host charge elapsed at least 750 ms, recent state, and a still-held unfired gesture. Visual interpolation alone cannot satisfy it. Release, switch, hide, lifecycle and focus/lock loss cancel the local gesture. The independently executed thirteen-group client proof includes old-press delay, missing acknowledgement and visual-ready/host-not-ready negatives.
- VERIFIED — Host reload alone transfers reserve to magazine. Controller timer expiry cannot refill, legacy total inventory cannot reconstruct a fresh magazine, and pending shot debits clear from exact acknowledgements rather than a high-water inference. `HostWeaponState` bounds reload history and fails closed before its evicted-history floor. Its spend path checks equipped-at-fire time, reload intervals, magazine and charge before decrement.
- VERIFIED — Death drops preserve magazine plus reserve. During the existing 275 ms admissible death-trade window, manual and automatic pickup remain blocked. An admitted late primary trade decrements the matching owner/life/weapon drop exactly once; duplicate claims do not reach spend, and sidearm trades cannot debit the primary drop. Post-window swap preserves the outgoing and incoming splits; scavenge adds only available rounds to reserve. Reviewed the independent authority verifier's actual-host late-trade, boundary pickup, swap-back and sidearm assertions; no new contradiction found in those paths.
- VERIFIED — Guest resume starts its wire shot base above both admitted and resolved/refused high-water, filters old-domain acknowledgements and subtracts only the shot base. Driver-returned intent IDs remain absolute. HostDriver calls trusted charge-only cancellation before authenticated resume facts and on its existing disconnected-seat edge. This removes old charge tickets while preserving reload deadlines, ammo and intent sequences.
- VERIFIED — Root main forwards the assigned intent sequence through the session facade and applies private weapon state after ordnance/spawn presentation. Its blur and pointer-lock loss listeners cancel immediately; frame input admission also considers active life, menu, focus and possession. Public bodies carry only active weapon ID. Private weapon state and remaining-total ordnance inventory target the owning seat.
