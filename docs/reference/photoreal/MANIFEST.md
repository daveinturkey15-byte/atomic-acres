# Photoreal Look-Target Manifest — Atomic Acres

Generated reference photographic studies for Three.js WebGPU renderer calibration.
All images share the identical lighting condition:
- **Solar Rig**: Harsh, high (~2:00 PM), slightly warm sun (elevation ~60°, direct desert solar intensity).
- **Skylight Rig**: Cloudless deep desert sky providing intense diffuse blue skylight fill (`#7fa2c8` ambient wash) into shaded faces and occlusions; shadows remain open and blue-tinted, never crushed or black.
- **Exposure**: Realistic neutral camera exposure; unclipped specular highlights and readable shadow detail without cinematic grade or tone-mapping crush.
- **Resolution**: 1376 × 768 PNG.
