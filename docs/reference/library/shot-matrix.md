## The frozen style block

Every generated reference in this catalogue uses this block **byte-identical**.
Only the subject sentence changes. Changing the block is a catalogue revision.


**id `AA-STYLE-2026-09-18`**


```
Original concept photograph for a fan game. Subject: an invented 1960s American 'world of tomorrow' nuclear-test show town in the Nevada desert - Googie and streamline-moderne architecture, bleached concrete, mown lawns, saturated accent colours on cream and terracotta stucco. Light: hard high slightly-warm afternoon sun about 60 degrees elevation, intense diffuse blue skylight fill into every shaded face so shadows stay open and blue-tinted and never crush to black, clear cloudless desert sky, hazy dry mountains on the horizon. Camera: full-frame stills lens, realistic neutral exposure, unclipped speculars, readable shadow detail, no cinematic grade.
```

**Negative block, appended to every prompt:**


```
No logos, no brand marks, no readable signage, no real-world trademarks, no game HUD, no watermark, no text overlay, no people's faces, no copyrighted character or weapon designs, no 1950s tract housing, no school buses, no snow, no rain, no night.
```

**Light slots** (the first is the fixed condition; the others are variations a
lane may request explicitly):

- `afternoon` - hard high slightly-warm afternoon sun (the fixed condition)
- `lowsun` - golden-hour low raking sun, long shadows (variation slot)
- `overcast` - high thin overcast, soft shadowless fill (variation slot)
- `interior` - interior lit only by window sun patch plus blue skylight bounce

Budget the subject sentence to about 60 words. CLIP-family text encoders truncate
silently at 77 tokens, and a subject named after the cut never renders.
